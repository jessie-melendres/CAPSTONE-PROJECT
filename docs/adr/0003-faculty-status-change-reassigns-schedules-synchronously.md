# Moving a faculty member off Teaching status reassigns their classes synchronously, not asynchronously

`schedules.faculty_id` is a required (`NOT NULL`) foreign key, so a faculty member going `On leave`/`Inactive` can't simply leave their existing `Schedule`s pointing at a non-teaching instructor, and can't be left unassigned either without a schema change. We considered making `faculty_id` nullable and letting an admin fix up "Unassigned" schedules later, but that ripples into every place that assumes a schedule has a faculty member (conflict checking, the faculty "credited to your account" authorization check, view labels) — a much larger, riskier change. Instead, `FacultyService::updateStatus()` requires a replacement faculty member (who must themselves be `Teaching`, and whose own timetable must not conflict with the classes being moved) to be chosen in the same action that changes the status; the reassignment and the status change happen together, atomically, in `AdminController::facultyUpdate`. A faculty member with no current classes needs no replacement. This matches how the change would actually happen at a real institution — a substitute is lined up before the leave takes effect, not after.
