# Remark is decided by the semestral average, not the final term alone

`Grade::computeRemark()` originally decided PASSED/FAILED from `final_grade` alone, ignoring `prelim_grade`/`midterm_grade` entirely — while a separate `semestralAverage()` (the mean of all three terms) was already being used elsewhere to compute the student's General Average. This meant a student could fail a subject on the strength of one bad final-period score despite a passing average, or vice versa. We decided the Remark should be derived from the Semestral Average instead, matching how the General Average already works and how the Gantt roadmap describes grade calculation as spanning all three terms. As a consequence, a Grade is now INCOMPLETE whenever *any* of the three terms is missing, not just the final one.
